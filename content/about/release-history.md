Versions are numbered MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH (for example, 0.1.0).

#### v0.1.0 — 2026-11-17

- Initial public release.
- 14 mouse bulk RNA-seq datasets, reprocessed with a standardized pipeline.
- Gene search from the home page, with typo-tolerant matching.
- Dataset filters for organism, underlying variant, sample type, sex, and age.
- Interactive transcripts per million (TPM) violin plots by experimental
  group, with optional Mann-Whitney or Kruskal-Wallis tests.
- Accessible data-table view of each plot, plus PNG and CSV downloads.

#### Planned: v0.2.0

- Human bulk RNA-seq datasets, processed with the same pipeline as v0.1.0.
