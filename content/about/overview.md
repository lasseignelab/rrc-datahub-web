The Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) Research Resource Consortium (RRC)
DataHub is a free, web-based resource of publicly available PKD datasets,
uniformly reprocessed with a standardized analysis
[pipeline](https://github.com/lasseignelab/rrc-datahub). Datasets can be
explored side by side without each user having to locate, download, process,
and vet the raw data themselves. No account or login is required.

The DataHub is part of a broader effort to reduce barriers to incorporating
genomics in PKD research, increase rigor and reproducibility by harmonizing
PKD bioinformatics resources, and release web-based analytical tools that
address outstanding questions in the PKD field.

A point-and-click interface lets you search for a gene, filter datasets by
study characteristics (organism, underlying variant, sample type, sex, and
age), and visualize results across experimental groups. No coding or
bioinformatics expertise is required.

The DataHub is developed by the Lasseigne Lab at the University of Alabama at
Birmingham (UAB).

**Who it is for:** PKD researchers and clinicians who want to check whether a
gene of interest differs across published PKD models, generate hypotheses, or
pick datasets for follow-up analysis.
