> Soelter, T.M., Crumley, A.B., Casper, K.J., Okunbor, E., Lasseigne, B.N.

release (v0.1.0:

Please also cite the original study for each dataset you use (see Datasets
and accessions), and the [nf-core/rnaseq](https://nf-co.re/rnaseq) pipeline.
